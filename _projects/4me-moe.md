## One structure, any scale

A standardised, stackable bay independent of any specific chassis, retrofittable onto a mixed fleet through a simple attachment system. Two rear doors open the full width, a lateral sliding door gives mid-route access, and a motorised floor rail brings packages to the opening — no climbing inside required.

## AGADEXO

A wearable exoskeleton built as a structural component of the vehicle, replacing the passenger seat. A single hydraulic arm activates when the cab door opens: the unit pivots out and descends to standing height, the driver steps in without bending or reaching, the door closes, mechanism resets — under ten seconds. Three quick-release buckles allow emergency removal, any single one sufficient to disengage the unit.

## The app

The mental model is ride-hailing applied to parcels: users book a vehicle rather than fill a shipping form, and live tracking shows where the parcel is right now rather than where it's been. Three flows — book, track, return.
