A complete product design system for Sammontana, the Italian ice cream brand founded in 1946. The brief was to redesign their retail presence — fridges, signage, merchandise, and packaging — so that each piece could adapt to any point of sale without losing brand identity. The answer was the cylinder: the same form as the logo, used as both structure and language across every object in the system.

## The idea

Sammontana's circular logo became the generative form for the entire project. Cylindrical geometry is not just aesthetic — it drives function. Fridge shells, shelf elements, bag straps, packaging profiles: all derive from the same round logic. The result is a family of objects that reads as a system without looking like a catalogue.

## The problem

Every retailer — from a beach kiosk to a flagship store — receives the same fridge. The result is a brand that always looks slightly wrong wherever it lands. A classic white chest freezer sits awkwardly in a premium shop; a garishly branded totem disappears in a traditional gelateria. The system needed to adapt, not just decorate.

Four products share the same cylindrical design language but address completely different spatial and commercial contexts.

<h3>Orzino — Compact horizontal</h3>

Same logic as Orzi, reduced for kiosks, beach bars, and temporary corners. Footprint of 944 mm wide, direct product visibility maintained, same yellow circular accent detail.

<h3>Expo — Display fridge</h3>

A rotating showcase fridge for flagship stores where presentation matters more than throughput. The interior runs on a central structural tube: three interchangeable shelf types — flat leaf, open bowl, and flat tray — slot on and rotate 360°. The bowl configuration has a detachable mechanism for one-handed product access.

## Merchandise

### La borsa

A structured summer bag designed around Sammontana's product dimensions. Internal pockets sized to Gruvi multipacks, elasticated slots sized to barattolini, and larger compartments for full box formats. Shoulder straps are single-fibre webbing, removable and interchangeable between all colourway variants — which match the barattolini palette exactly, making cross-swapping an intentional social mechanic.

### Lingua

A spring-form utensil that functions as a gelato spoon, a food tong, and a phone stand. Sold in sets of three so all three functions can be in use simultaneously. Small enough to live in the bag, useful enough to be taken out again.

### Gusta

A ground-stake support for hands-free gelato eating in beach contexts. Accepts cones, cups, and sticks. Includes a small spoon dock and a phone ledge. Folds compact. Intended to make a Sammontana product the centre of the pause, not just the excuse for it.

## Packaging

The Gruvi was used as a case study for rethinking single-product packaging. The existing format wastes material at the corners of a fundamentally cylindrical object. The new proposal wraps the same product in a recyclable cardboard cylinder — same format as the barattolino — eliminating dead space, reducing plastic, and making the packaging itself communicate the brand through form rather than print alone.
